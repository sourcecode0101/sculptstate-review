THE STUDIO

A space to move with intention — and intensity.

Sculpt State facilitates a hybrid of reformer-informed pilates and strength training, infused with elements of sweat and sound. A place to cultivate real, visible change — and to teach the method to a global audience.

The Method

Precision and power in one session. Controlled pilates movement layered with progressive strength — designed to reshape how you train, look and feel. Gritty in energy, refined in delivery.

The Energy

Chrome and concrete. Low light and live sound. An LA-warehouse atmosphere held to studio-grade discipline — high intensity without ever losing control.
